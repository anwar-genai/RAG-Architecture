THE RECALL → PRECISION FUNNEL the tradeoff that decides answer quality · cast wide, then cut hard

Every stage trades recall for precision. Retrieve too few candidates and the answer was never in the running; put too many in the prompt and the model loses the one that mattered. Recall is won in retrieval and can never be recovered later; precision is won in reranking and assembly.
